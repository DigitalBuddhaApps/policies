2. COPPA Compliance (Children Under 13)

This app is designed to be safe for children under 13 years of age. We fully comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) requirements:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect any personal information from children or adults under any circumstances
  • Child-Directed Treatment: All advertisements shown through Google AdMob are configured for child-directed treatment
  • Non-Personalized Ads Only: We only display non-personalized, age-appropriate advertisements that comply with COPPA standards
  • No Behavioral Tracking: We do not track user behavior, create user profiles, or use persistent identifiers for tracking
  • Parental Consent Not Required: Since we collect absolutely no personal information, verifiable parental consent is not required
  • No Third-Party Data Sharing: We do not share any user data with third parties (except anonymous ad delivery as described below)

3. Advertising

Pureland Songs 2 displays advertisements through Google AdMob to support free access to Pure Land Buddhist songs, chants, and prayers. Our advertising practices are fully COPPA-compliant:

  • Non-Personalized Ads: All advertisements displayed in this app are non-personalized and do not use cookies, persistent device identifiers, or any form of behavioral tracking
  • Age-Appropriate Content: All ads are restricted to General Audiences (rated G) content, suitable for all ages including young children
  • Child-Directed Treatment Flag: Google AdMob is configured with the child-directed treatment flag, ensuring COPPA compliance for all ad requests
  • Limited Technical Data: Google AdMob may collect minimal technical information (such as device type, operating system version, and app version) solely for the purpose of delivering advertisements, but this data is not linked to any personal information
  • No Ad Personalization: We have disabled all personalized advertising features in AdMob

For more detailed information about Google AdMob's privacy practices and COPPA compliance, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service integrated into this app is Google AdMob for displaying advertisements.

Google AdMob is configured with the following privacy-protective settings:

  • Child-directed treatment enabled
  • Personalized advertising disabled
  • COPPA compliance mode active
  • Age-appropriate ad content filtering enabled

No other third-party services, analytics tools, or tracking systems are used in this app.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, there is no personal data at risk. The app functions entirely offline except when loading advertisements from Google AdMob.

All Buddhist songs, chants, and related content are stored locally on the user's device and do not require an internet connection to access.
