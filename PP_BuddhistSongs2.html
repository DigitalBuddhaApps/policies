Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2024

Important Notice: This app is designed for children under 13 years old. We are committed to protecting children's privacy and complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

1. Information We Collect

Buddhist Songs (2) does NOT collect, store, or share any personal information from users, including children. We do not require registration, login, or any form of personal data input.

2. Target Audience

This app is specifically designed and intended for children under 13 years old. The content consists of Buddhist songs and spiritual content suitable for all ages, particularly children and families.

3. Advertising

This app displays advertisements through Google AdMob. All advertising is configured to be COPPA-compliant:

  • Child-Directed Treatment: All ads are tagged for child-directed treatment
  • Non-Personalized Ads: We do not collect or use personal information for ad targeting
  • General Audiences Only: Ad content is limited to G-rated (General Audiences) material
  • Age-Appropriate: Ads suitable for children under 13

4. Third-Party Services

We use Google AdMob for advertising purposes. AdMob may collect certain device information to serve non-personalized ads:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • IP address (for geographic region only, not tracking)
  • App usage data (anonymous)

This information is collected and processed by Google in accordance with their privacy policies. No personal information is collected or shared.

5. Data Storage

The app stores only minimal local data on your device:

  • Song playback preferences
  • Text size settings
  • Language preferences

All data remains on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

6. Permissions

The app requires the following permissions:

  • Internet: Required for displaying ads only
  • Set Wallpaper: Optional, only used when you choose to set a Buddhist image as wallpaper

7. COPPA Compliance

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • No collection of personal information from children
  • No tracking or profiling of children
  • No personalized advertising
  • No third-party data sharing
  • Age-appropriate content only
  • Parental notification through this policy

8. Parental Rights

Since we do not collect any personal information, there is no data to review, modify, or delete. Parents can:

  • Supervise their child's use of the app
  • Uninstall the app at any time to remove all local data
  • Contact us with any concerns about child safety

9. External Links

The app may contain links to:

  • Digital Buddha Apps website
  • Google Play Store
  • Social media pages (Facebook, YouTube)

These external sites have their own privacy policies. We recommend parental supervision when children access external links.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
