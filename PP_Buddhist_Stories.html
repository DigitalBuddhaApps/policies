Privacy Policy

Buddhist Stories (25-in-1)
Important Notice: This app is designed for users of all ages, including children under 13. We are committed to protecting the privacy of all our users, especially children. This privacy policy complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and other applicable privacy laws.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

We collect minimal personal information to provide and improve our services:

  • App Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve our app functionality and user experience
  • Device Information: Basic device information (device type, operating system version) for app functionality and compatibility
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal identifiers
  • No Location Data: We do not collect precise location information
  • No Biometric Data: We do not collect fingerprints, facial recognition data, or other biometric information

Children's Information (COPPA Compliance)

For users under 13 years of age, we have special protections in place:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent
  • If we discover we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately
  • Parents can contact us to review, modify, or delete their child's information
  • We do not use behavioral advertising for children under 13
  • We do not share children's information with third parties except as necessary for app functionality

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • Provide and improve our app services and user experience
  • Display relevant advertisements through Google AdMob (with age-appropriate content)
  • Analyze app performance and user experience to enhance functionality
  • Ensure app security and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

3. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

Google AdMob

Our app uses Google AdMob for advertising services. AdMob may collect:

  • Device advertising identifiers (which can be reset by users)
  • App usage data for ad personalization (with appropriate age restrictions)
  • General location data (country/region level, not precise location)
  • Device information for ad delivery optimization

Important: For more information about Google's privacy practices, visit: Google Privacy Policy

Data Sharing Policy

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations or court orders
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users
  • With service providers who assist us in app operations (under strict confidentiality agreements)

4. Data Security and Protection

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: Data encryption in transit and at rest using industry-standard protocols
  • Access Controls: Limited access to personal information on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular Audits: Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Secure Storage: Secure data storage practices with appropriate backup and recovery procedures
  • Staff Training: Regular privacy and security training for all staff members

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request access to your personal information
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Portability: Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Opt-out: Opt-out of data collection for advertising purposes
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

For Parents of Children Under 13

Parents have additional rights under COPPA:

  • Review and delete their child's personal information
  • Refuse further collection of their child's information
  • Request a description of the types of information collected about their child

6. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Usage Data: Up to 2 years for analytics and improvement purposes
  • Analytics Data: Up to 1 year for performance analysis
  • Children's Data: Deleted immediately upon request or when no longer needed
  • Legal Requirements: Some data may be retained longer to comply with legal obligations

7. International Users and Data Transfers

If you are located outside the United States, please note that:

  • Your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States
  • We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international data transfers
  • We comply with applicable international privacy laws including GDPR for EU users
  • You have the same rights regardless of your location

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify users of any material changes through:

  • App updates with notification of policy changes
  • In-app notifications about significant changes
  • Email notifications (if we have your email address)
  • Prominent notice on our website (if applicable)

Continued Use: Your continued use of the app after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

9. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know: Know what personal information is collected, used, and shared
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information
  • Right to Opt-out: Opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Right to Non-discrimination: Not be discriminated against for exercising privacy rights
