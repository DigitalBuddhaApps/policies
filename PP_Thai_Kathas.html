1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information We DO NOT Collect

We are committed to protecting your privacy and do not collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates, precise location, or location history
  • Contact Information: Contact lists, address books, or social media connections
  • Media Files: Photos, videos, or audio recordings from your device
  • Account Information: Social media account details or login credentials
  • Children's Personal Data: Any personally identifiable information from children under 13
  • Sensitive Information: Financial data, health information, or biometric data

1.2 Information We May Collect

We only collect anonymous, non-personal information necessary for app functionality:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for analytics purposes
  • Usage Analytics: App usage statistics, feature interactions, and crash reports to improve the app
  • Advertising Data: Anonymous advertising identifiers for displaying relevant ads
  • Performance Data: App performance metrics and error logs for technical improvements

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide and improve our app functionality and user experience
  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdMob
  • To analyze app performance and identify areas for improvement
  • To fix bugs and enhance app features
  • To ensure app security and prevent fraudulent activities
  • To comply with legal requirements and industry standards
  • To provide customer support when needed

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within our app. AdMob may collect:

  • Anonymous advertising identifiers
  • Usage data for ad personalization
  • Device information for ad targeting

For detailed information about Google's data practices, please review their Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.2 Analytics Services

We may use analytics services to understand how our app is used. These services collect:

  • Anonymous usage statistics
  • App performance metrics
  • Crash reports and error logs

All data collected is anonymized and cannot be used to identify individual users.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Special Protection for Children Under 13

We take children's privacy seriously and comply with COPPA regulations:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • Immediate Deletion: If we discover that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately
  • Parental Rights: Parents can contact us to review, delete, or refuse further collection of their child's information
  • Anonymous Data Only: We only collect anonymous usage data necessary for app functionality
  • Age-Appropriate Advertising: All advertising is age-appropriate and complies with children's advertising standards
  • No Behavioral Tracking: We do not track children's behavior across apps or websites
  • Parental Consent: We do not require parental consent for anonymous data collection

5. Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect any data we collect:

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard encryption protocols
  • Access Control: Access to data is limited to authorized personnel only
  • Regular Audits: We conduct regular security audits and updates
  • No Personal Storage: No personal information is stored on our servers
  • Secure Infrastructure: We use secure cloud infrastructure with proper safeguards
  • Incident Response: We have procedures in place to respond to any security incidents

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your information with third parties, except in the following limited circumstances:

  • Advertising Partners: With Google AdMob for ad delivery (anonymous data only)
  • Analytics Providers: With analytics services for app improvement (anonymous data only)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfers: In case of app sale or merger (with notice to users)
  • Service Providers: With trusted service providers who assist in app operations (under strict confidentiality agreements)

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your information:

  • Right to Know: Right to know what information we collect and how we use it
  • Right to Access: Right to access your information (though we collect minimal data)
  • Right to Delete: Right to delete your information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Right to opt-out of data collection (where applicable)
  • Right to Contact: Right to contact us with privacy concerns
  • Right to Complain: Right to file a complaint with relevant authorities

8. International Users

If you are located outside the United States, please note that:

  • Information we collect may be transferred to and processed in the United States
  • By using our app, you consent to this transfer
  • We comply with applicable international data protection laws
  • We implement appropriate safeguards for international data transfers

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy in the app
  • Updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy
  • Providing notice through the app interface
  • Maintaining version history of policy changes

Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
