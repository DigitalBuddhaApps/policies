👶 Children's Privacy Notice

Our app is designed for users of all ages, including children under 13. We take special care to protect children's privacy and comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) requirements.

1. Information We Collect

Heart Sutra (Sanskrit) is a spiritual application that respects your privacy. We collect minimal information necessary for app functionality:

  • App usage analytics (anonymized and aggregated)
  • Device information for compatibility and performance optimization
  • Crash reports for app improvement and bug fixes
  • Advertising identifiers for personalized ads (with user consent)
  • Audio session data for background playback functionality
For Children Under 13: We do not collect any personal information from children without verifiable parental consent. Any data collection is limited to app functionality only.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Provide better spiritual content and features
  • Ensure compatibility across different devices
  • Generate anonymous usage statistics
  • Deliver relevant advertisements (with user consent)

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. We may share information only when:

  • Required by law or legal process
  • Necessary for app functionality (with trusted service providers)
  • With your explicit consent
  • To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • Encrypted data transmission
  • Secure data storage
  • Regular security assessments
  • Limited access to personal data

5. Third-Party Services

Our app may use third-party services for:

  • Analytics: Google Analytics (anonymized data only)
  • Advertising: Google AdMob (with consent management)
  • Crash reporting: Firebase Crashlytics
  • Performance monitoring: Firebase Performance

These services have their own privacy policies and data practices.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Special Protection for Children Under 13:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • Any data collection is limited to app functionality only
  • We do not use behavioral advertising for children
  • Parents can contact us to review or delete their child's information
  • We comply with all COPPA requirements

7. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy:
