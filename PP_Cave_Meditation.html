⚠️ Important Notice: This app is designed for users of all ages, including children under 13. We are committed to protecting the privacy of all our users, especially children. Our app complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and other applicable privacy laws.

1. 📊 Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide you with the best meditation experience:

  • 📈 App Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve our app performance and user experience
  • 📱 Device Information: Basic device information such as device type, operating system version, and screen resolution
  • 📍 Location Data: Only if you choose to use location-based features (completely optional and can be disabled)
  • 🎵 Audio Data: Local audio files for meditation sessions (stored locally on your device, never transmitted)
  • ⚙️ App Settings: Your meditation preferences and app configuration settings

2. 🎯 How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Provide and continuously improve our meditation services
  • Personalize your meditation experience based on your preferences
  • Ensure optimal app functionality and performance
  • Provide responsive customer support when needed
  • Analyze app usage patterns to enhance user experience
  • Ensure compliance with applicable laws and regulations

3. 🤝 Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except in the following limited circumstances:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users
  • With your explicit written consent
  • In case of a business transfer (merger, acquisition, etc.) with prior notice

4. 👶 Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

🛡️ Special Protection for Children Under 13:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent
  • If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately
  • Parents can contact us at any time to review, delete, or refuse further collection of their child's information
  • We encourage parents to monitor their children's use of our app and discuss online privacy
  • Our app is designed to be safe for children with appropriate content and no inappropriate advertising
  • We do not collect more information than is reasonably necessary for the app's functionality

5. 🔐 Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your information:

  • End-to-end encryption of sensitive data
  • Secure data transmission using industry-standard protocols
  • Regular security assessments and vulnerability testing
  • Limited access to personal information on a need-to-know basis
  • Secure data storage with regular backups
  • Employee training on data protection and privacy

Our app may use third-party services for enhanced functionality:

  • 📊 Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics with privacy-focused settings)
  • 📢 Advertising: Non-personalized, age-appropriate ads (Google AdMob with COPPA compliance)

These services have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review. We ensure all third-party services comply with applicable privacy laws.

  • 🗑️ Deletion: Request deletion of your information
  • 🚫 Opt-out: Opt-out of data collection and processing

  • 🔄 Withdrawal: Withdraw consent at any time
  • 📝 Objection: Object to certain types of data processing

We retain your information only as long as necessary to:
