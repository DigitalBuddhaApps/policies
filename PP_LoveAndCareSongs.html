1. Introduction

Welcome to Love and Care Songs ("we," "our," or "the App"). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information collection, use, and disclosure when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with all applicable privacy laws, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and other international privacy regulations.

2. Target Audience and COPPA Compliance

Our Target Audience

This application is designed for users of all ages, including children under 13 years old. As such, we have implemented strict COPPA-compliant practices to ensure the safety and privacy of all users, especially children.

COPPA Compliance Measures:

  • No Personal Information Collection: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from any user, including children
  • No Registration Required: The app does not require users to create accounts or provide any personal data
  • Child-Safe Advertising: All advertisements are configured as child-directed with appropriate content ratings
  • No Behavioral Tracking: We do not track user behavior or create user profiles
  • No Third-Party Data Sharing: We do not share any user information with third parties for marketing purposes

3. Information We DO NOT Collect

We want to be absolutely clear about what information we DO NOT collect:

  • Personal Identifiers: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates, precise location, or geolocation data
  • Device Identifiers: Device IDs used for tracking or profiling purposes
  • Media Files: Photos, videos, audio recordings, or camera/microphone access
  • Contacts & Calendar: Access to contacts, calendar, or other personal data
  • User-Generated Content: Comments, reviews, or any user input
  • Browsing History: Websites visited or app usage patterns
  • Payment Information: Credit card numbers or financial data

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Child-Directed Advertising Configuration

Our app displays advertisements through Google AdMob, which has been specifically configured for child-directed treatment in compliance with COPPA:

AdMob COPPA Settings:

  • Child-Directed Treatment: ENABLED - All ad requests are tagged as child-directed
  • Maximum Ad Content Rating: G (General Audiences) - Only family-friendly content
  • Under Age of Consent: ENABLED - All users treated as under age of consent
  • Personalized Ads: DISABLED - No behavioral or interest-based advertising
  • Ad Frequency Control: Limited to 8 ads per session with 5-minute minimum intervals

What Google AdMob May Collect (Non-Personal):

  • Device Type & OS: General device information for ad compatibility (e.g., "Android phone")
  • General Location: Country or region-level location only (not precise GPS)
  • Ad Interaction: Anonymous data about ad views and clicks (no user identification)
  • Technical Data: Screen size, language settings (for ad formatting only)

Important: All data collected by AdMob is anonymous and cannot be used to identify individual users. Google's child-directed ad serving ensures compliance with COPPA regulations.

For more information about Google AdMob's privacy practices and COPPA compliance, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob COPPA: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6223431

5. Facebook SDK (Mediation Only)

Facebook SDK is integrated solely for ad mediation purposes through Google AdMob. This means:

  • Facebook Audience Network ads may appear through AdMob mediation
  • All ads are configured as non-personalized to comply with COPPA
  • Facebook may collect limited technical data for ad delivery (device type, ad performance)
  • No personal information or user profiles are created
  • Facebook's data collection is restricted to COPPA-compliant, non-personal technical data

6. How the App Functions

Love and Care Songs is primarily an offline application:

  • Content Playback: All songs and content are stored locally in the app and play offline
  • No Cloud Storage: We do not use cloud services or remote servers for user data
  • Internet Required For: Displaying advertisements only
  • Offline Mode: Core functionality works without internet connection

7. Data Security

Since we do not collect any personal information, there is minimal security risk:

  • No personal data is stored on our servers (we don't have servers for user data)
  • No user accounts or login credentials to secure
  • App preferences are stored locally on the device only
  • We use industry-standard security practices for app delivery through Google Play Store
