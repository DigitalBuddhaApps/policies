Kids Buddhist Songs – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2025

1. Overview

Kids Buddhist Songs is produced by Digital Buddha Apps (“we,” “us,” or “our”). We design this app for children and families and take privacy seriously. This document explains how the app handles information when you use it.

2. Information We Collect

  • No personal data: We do not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • Device data for ads only: The app integrates Google AdMob to display non-personalized advertisements. AdMob may receive limited diagnostic data (such as coarse location, device identifiers, or IP address) strictly to deliver child-safe ads and prevent fraud.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected through AdMob is used only to show contextual, family-friendly ads, cap ad frequency, and combat abuse. We do not store, sell, or share data with any other third parties.

4. Ads and the Families Policy

All ad placements are tagged for the Google Families Ads Program, limited to G-rated creatives, and configured to serve non-personalized ads. For more details on how Google handles information in ads, visit https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. Children’s Privacy

We comply with COPPA and Google Play Families Policy Requirements. If you believe we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

6. Data Security

We do not maintain our own backend servers for this app, and all audio assets reside locally within the app package. AdMob handles its network traffic over encrypted HTTPS connections.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect new features or regulatory guidance. Updates will include a new “Last updated” date and will be distributed with future app releases.
