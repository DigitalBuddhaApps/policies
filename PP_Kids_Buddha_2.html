Privacy Policy

Kids Buddha App

Developer: Big Kids Apps

Package Name: com.bigkidsapps.kidsbuddha2

Target Audience: Children under 5 years old to adults

Important Notice: This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our Kids Buddha app. Since our app is designed for children and families, we take privacy protection very seriously and follow strict guidelines to ensure the safety of all users, especially children.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

Our Kids Buddha app is designed with privacy in mind. We do NOT collect:

  • Personal information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Photos or videos from your device
  • Contact lists or address books
  • Microphone recordings (except for the audio playback of Buddhist mantras)
  • Camera access or photos
  • Social media information
  • Biometric data

1.2 Information We May Collect

The following information may be collected for app functionality and improvement:

  • Device Information: Basic device information such as device model, operating system version, and app version for compatibility purposes
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics to understand how the app is used (e.g., which mantras are played most frequently)
  • Crash Reports: Technical information about app crashes to help us fix bugs and improve stability
  • Advertising Data: Information related to displaying advertisements (see Advertising section below)

2. How We Use Information

We use the limited information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the app's functionality
  • To improve app performance and user experience
  • To fix bugs and technical issues
  • To display relevant advertisements (see Advertising section)
  • To ensure the app works properly on different devices

3. Advertising

Our app includes advertisements through Google AdMob to support the free version of the app. Here's what you need to know:

3.1 Google AdMob

  • We use Google AdMob to display banner advertisements
  • AdMob may collect anonymous advertising identifiers and device information
  • AdMob follows Google's privacy policies and advertising guidelines
  • We do not have access to personal information collected by AdMob

3.2 Ad Personalization

Advertisements may be personalized based on:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • General location (country/region level, not precise location)
  • App usage patterns

3.3 Children's Privacy and Advertising

We are committed to protecting children's privacy:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • Advertisements are filtered to be appropriate for all ages
  • We follow COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) guidelines

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Audio files for Buddhist mantras and chants
  • Text files for mantra lyrics in different languages (Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Pinyin)
  • App settings and preferences
  • Wallpaper images (if you choose to set them)

4.2 Security Measures

  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • We do not transmit personal information to external servers
  • Audio and text files are embedded in the app and do not require internet access
  • We use industry-standard security practices

5. Permissions

Our app requests the following permissions for functionality:

  • Internet Access: To display advertisements and check for app updates
  • Network State: To determine internet connectivity for ads
  • WiFi State: To optimize ad delivery
  • Wake Lock: To keep the app running while playing mantras
  • Write External Storage: To save ringtones to your device
  • Write Settings: To set ringtones as your device's ringtone
  • Set Wallpaper: To set Buddhist images as your device wallpaper

6. Third-Party Services

6.1 Google Services

  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements
  • Google Play Services: For app functionality and updates

6.2 Third-Party Privacy Policies

When using third-party services, their privacy policies apply:

  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Store: Google Privacy Policy

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Since our app is designed for children and families, we follow strict guidelines:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • If we discover we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately
  • Parents can contact us to review, update, or delete their child's information
  • We do not require children to provide personal information to use the app
  • All content is appropriate for children and families

8. Your Rights and Choices

8.1 Your Rights

  • Right to access your information
  • Right to correct inaccurate information
  • Right to delete your information
  • Right to opt-out of data collection
  • Right to data portability

8.2 How to Exercise Your Rights

  • Contact us using the information provided below
  • Uninstall the app to stop data collection
  • Disable advertising personalization in your device settings

9. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary:

  • Usage analytics are retained for up to 2 years
  • Crash reports are retained for up to 1 year
  • Local app data is retained until you uninstall the app

10. International Users

  • We comply with applicable international privacy laws
  • EU users have additional rights under GDPR

  • Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy

For users in the European Union, our legal basis for processing information includes:

  • Legitimate Interest: To provide and improve our app services
