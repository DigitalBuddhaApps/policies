Important Notice: Kids Buddhist Songs is designed for infants, toddlers, and young children (including ages 0–5). We comply with COPPA and Google Play Families Policy requirements for child-directed experiences.

1. Information We Collect

Kids Buddhist Songs does not collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information (PII). No accounts, usernames, photos, cameras, microphones, or location data are requested.

2. Target Audience

The entire experience is curated for toddlers and preschoolers (ages 0–5) and their families. There is no user-generated content, chat, or sharing inside the child flows.

3. Data Storage

The app stores only minimal local data on your device:

  • Song playback preferences
  • Text size settings
  • Language preferences

All data remains on your device and is never sent to external servers.

4. Permissions

The app uses the following permissions:

  • Internet / Network State: Required solely to request Google AdMob ads.
  • Set Wallpaper / Wallpaper Hints: Lets parents apply bundled wallpapers. No online data transfer occurs.

5. Advertising & Monetization

To keep the app free while protecting children:

  • We use Google AdMob only. No other ad networks or third-party SDKs are integrated.
  • Ad requests are flagged with setTagForChildDirectedTreatment(TRUE) and setMaxAdContentRating(G), and all personalized/sensitive categories are disabled.
  • Ad formats include native banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. Interstitials are limited to one every two minutes to avoid disrupting young users.
  • Rewarded ads simply unlock bonus wallpapers. They never provide money, consumables, or in-app currency.
  • During development we use Google’s official test ad unit IDs. Production IDs retain the exact same child-directed configuration.

If you see an ad that seems inappropriate, tap the AdChoices icon to report it and contact us so we can block the category in AdMob.

6. COPPA / Google Play Families Compliance

We meet COPPA and Families Policy requirements by ensuring:

  • No personal data collection or profiling.
  • No social features, account creation, or external sharing within the child experience.
  • Ads and content remain consistent with a G-rated/toddler audience.
  • All sensitive ad categories (dating, gambling, medical, political, religious proselytizing, etc.) are blocked.

7. Parental Rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing to view or delete. Parents and guardians can:

  • Supervise and limit device usage as they see fit.
  • Uninstall the app at any time to remove all locally stored settings.
  • Contact us if they notice any ad, link, or feature that seems unsuitable.

8. External Links

The parent/guardian area may link to:

  • Digital Buddha Apps support email
  • Our Google Play developer page
  • Official Facebook or YouTube channels for parents

These links open outside the child experience and are intended for adults. Each destination has its own privacy policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect new features or regulatory guidance. The “Last updated” date will change whenever we publish a revision, and we will resubmit this policy to Google Play if requirements change.
