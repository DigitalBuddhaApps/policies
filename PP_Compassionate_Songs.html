⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTICE: This app is designed for a general audience and may be used by children under the age of 13. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

1. Information We Collect

Compassionate Songs ("we," "our," or "the app") is designed to respect your privacy. We do not directly collect, store, or process any personal information from our users, including children under 13.

2. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process certain information, including:

  • Device information: Device type, operating system version, device identifiers
  • Ad interaction data: Ad views, clicks, and interaction metrics
  • General location: Country and city level location (not precise GPS location)
  • IP address: Your device's IP address
  • App usage data: Time spent in app, features used

Children Under 13: We have configured AdMob to serve only age-appropriate, non-personalized ads when our app is used. We do NOT enable personalized advertising for child-directed content. We comply with Google's policies for serving ads to children.

For more information about how Google handles data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob and Data Collection: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • How Google uses data from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Permissions

Our app requests the following permissions:

  • Internet Access: Required to display advertisements and any online content
  • Set Wallpaper: Allows users to optionally set images from the app as their device wallpaper (user-initiated only, completely optional)

What we DON'T request:

  • ❌ Access to contacts
  • ❌ Access to camera or microphone
  • ❌ Precise location tracking
  • ❌ Access to photos or media files (except for wallpaper feature)
  • ❌ Phone state or call information
  • ❌ Any other sensitive personal information

4. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal information on our servers, there is minimal risk to user data. All app functionality works locally on your device. Any data collected by third-party services (like AdMob) is governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We take children's privacy very seriously and are committed to protecting it:

  • ✅ We do NOT knowingly collect, use, or share personal information from children under 13
  • ✅ We do NOT require registration, account creation, or any personal information to use the app
  • ✅ We do NOT enable personalized ads for child-directed content
  • ✅ We do NOT share any personal information with third parties except as described in this policy (AdMob for non-personalized ads only)
