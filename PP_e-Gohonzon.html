1. Information We Collect

e-Gohonzon is a sacred Buddhist spiritual application that respects your privacy and the sanctity of your spiritual practice. We collect minimal information necessary for app functionality:

Data We Collect:

  • Anonymous app usage analytics - To improve your experience and app performance
  • Device information - For optimal performance and compatibility across different devices
  • Audio settings and preferences - For karaoke features and personalization
  • Language preferences - For multilingual prayers and content delivery
  • Crash reports - For app stability improvement and bug fixes
  • Prayer session statistics - Stored locally on your device for personal tracking

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to enhance your spiritual experience and improve our services:

  • Improve app performance and audio quality
  • Optimize karaoke highlighting and timing
  • Provide better spiritual content in multiple languages
  • Fix technical issues and enhance stability
  • Ensure compatibility across different devices
  • Personalize your prayer and meditation experience
  • Develop new features that serve the spiritual community

3. Information Sharing

We deeply respect the privacy of your spiritual practice. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Information may only be shared when:

  • Required by law or legal process
  • Necessary for essential app functionality
  • With your explicit written consent
  • To protect the rights and safety of users
  • In case of emergency situations requiring immediate action

4. Data Security

We implement strong security measures to protect your information with the same reverence we hold for sacred texts. Your data is protected against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction through:

  • Encryption of sensitive data
  • Secure data transmission protocols
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and authentication measures
  • Secure data storage practices

5. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following services to enhance functionality:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying respectful advertisements that align with our spiritual values
  • Google Analytics: For understanding app usage patterns (completely anonymized data)
  • Crash Reporting: For identifying and fixing technical issues quickly

All third-party services are carefully chosen to respect the spiritual nature of our app and are configured to minimize data collection while maintaining functionality.

6. Audio and Karaoke Features

Our sacred audio features are designed with privacy and security in mind:

  • Audio files stored securely on your device
  • Karaoke lyrics and timing processed locally
  • Audio enhancement settings saved on your device
  • No audio content transmitted to external servers
  • Prayer recitation data kept private and local
  • No recording or storage of your voice or recitations

7. Spiritual Content and Languages

We support multiple languages for prayers to serve the global Buddhist community:

  • Content selection based on your language preference
  • Multilingual prayer texts stored locally
  • Cultural and religious content handled with respect
  • No tracking of specific prayers or spiritual practices
  • Support for 17 languages including English, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Vietnamese, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Russian, Thai, Portuguese, Indonesian, Bengali, Italian, and Dutch

8. Children's Privacy

Special Protection for Children Under 13:

  • e-Gohonzon is specifically designed for spiritual practitioners of all ages, including children under 13
  • Our app is family-friendly and safe for children to use with parental guidance
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent
  • Our content is appropriate for family spiritual practice and educational purposes
  • We take special care to ensure a safe, educational environment for young practitioners
  • Parents can monitor their children's use of the app
  • All content is educational and spiritually enriching
  • No social features or external communication capabilities
  • No in-app purchases or external links without parental oversight
  • Compliance with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) requirements
