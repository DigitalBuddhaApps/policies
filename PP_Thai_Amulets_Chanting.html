Privacy Policy - Thai Amulets Chanting

Effective Date: January 1, 2024

Last Updated: January 1, 2024

Important Notice: This app is designed for users of all ages, including children under 13. We are committed to protecting the privacy of all our users, especially children.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

  • Account Information: Email address (optional, for premium features)
  • Usage Data: App usage statistics, meditation session duration
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, app version

1.2 Information from Children Under 13

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • If we discover we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately
  • Parents can contact us to review, update, or delete their child's information

2. How We Use Your Information

  • App Functionality: To provide meditation and chanting features
  • Personalization: To customize your meditation experience
  • Analytics: To improve app performance and user experience
  • Support: To provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

3.1 Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements (with age-appropriate content)
  • Analytics Services: For app performance monitoring
  • Cloud Storage: For backing up your meditation progress (optional)

3.2 We Do NOT Share:

  • Personal information with third parties for marketing purposes
  • Children's information with any third parties
  • Your meditation data with advertisers

4. Data Security

  • We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data
  • All data transmission is secured using SSL/TLS protocols
  • We regularly update our security measures
  • Access to personal information is restricted to authorized personnel only

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Special Protection for Children: We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and take extra precautions to protect children's privacy.
  • We do not collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent
  • Parents can review, modify, or delete their child's information at any time
  • We provide clear notice to parents about our data practices
  • All advertisements shown to children are age-appropriate and educational

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 For All Users:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and data
  • Opt-out of data collection for analytics
  • Request a copy of your data

6.2 For Parents of Children Under 13:

  • Review all information collected from your child
  • Refuse to permit further collection of your child's information
  • Delete your child's information
  • Revoke consent at any time

7. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained until account deletion
  • Usage Analytics: Retained for 2 years maximum
  • Children's Data: Deleted immediately upon parental request
  • Meditation Progress: Retained until you choose to delete it

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data during such transfers.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance
