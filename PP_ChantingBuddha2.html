Privacy Policy for Chanting Buddha 2

Effective Date: October 2024

App Name: Chanting Buddha 2

Developer: Digital Buddha Apps

Contact Email: digitalbuddhaapps@gmail.com

⚠️ IMPORTANT: This app is designed for a general audience, including children under 13 years of age. We are fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and do not collect any personal information from users of any age.

1. Information We Collect

We DO NOT collect any personal information.

Chanting Buddha 2 does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information from users, including:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Physical addresses
  • Geolocation data
  • Photos or videos
  • Contacts
  • Device identifiers (beyond what's required for ad delivery)
  • Any other personal information

2. COPPA Compliance (Children Under 13)

This app is safe for children under 13 years of age. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) requirements:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect any personal information from children or adults
  • Child-Directed Treatment: All ads shown through Google AdMob are configured for child-directed treatment
  • Non-Personalized Ads: We only show non-personalized, age-appropriate advertisements
  • No Tracking: We do not track user behavior or create user profiles
  • No Cookies: We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies
  • Parental Consent Not Required: Since we collect no personal information, parental consent is not required

3. Advertising

Chanting Buddha 2 displays advertisements through Google AdMob to support free access to Buddhist chants and prayers. Our advertising is COPPA-compliant:

  • Non-Personalized Ads: All advertisements are non-personalized and do not use cookies or device identifiers for ad targeting
  • Age-Appropriate Content: Ads are restricted to General Audiences (rated G) content only
  • Child-Directed Treatment: AdMob is configured to treat all users as children under 13
  • Limited Data Collection: Google AdMob may collect limited technical information (device type, app version, OS version) solely for ad delivery, but does not collect personal information
  • No Behavioral Targeting: Ads are not based on user behavior or preferences

For more information about Google AdMob's privacy practices, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used in this app is Google AdMob for advertisements. Google AdMob is configured with child-directed treatment and COPPA compliance settings. We do not use any analytics, social media integrations, or other third-party services that collect personal information.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect any personal information, there is no personal data to secure. The app functions entirely offline except when displaying advertisements. All app content (audio files, texts, images) is stored locally on the device.

6. Permissions

Chanting Buddha 2 requires the following permissions:

  • Internet: Required only for displaying advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Set Wallpaper: Allows users to optionally set Buddha images as phone wallpaper (user-initiated only)

No other data is transmitted over the internet. No permissions are used to collect personal information.

7. User Rights

Since we do not collect any personal information:

  • There is no personal data to access, modify, or delete
  • No user accounts are created
  • No data is shared with third parties (except anonymous ad delivery)
  • No data is stored on external servers
  • Users can uninstall the app at any time to remove all app data

8. Data Retention

Since we do not collect any personal information, there is no data retention policy required. The app stores only:

  • Audio files for Buddhist chants
  • Text files for chant lyrics
  • Image files for Buddhist imagery

All content is stored locally on the device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

9. International Users

Chanting Buddha 2 is available worldwide. Since we do not collect any personal information, GDPR, CCPA, and other international privacy laws do not apply to data collection. Users from all countries can use this app safely.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. Any changes will be posted within the app and will be effective immediately upon posting. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Parental Rights (COPPA)

Parents and guardians have the following rights regarding children's use of this app:

  • Review: Since no personal information is collected, there is nothing to review
  • Delete: Since no personal information is collected, there is nothing to delete
  • Refuse Further Collection: No personal information is collected at any time
  • Supervision: Parents can supervise their children's use of this app without concerns about data collection

All content is appropriate for all ages, including children.
