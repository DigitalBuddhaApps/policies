Privacy Policy

Kids Buddhist Songs (English) for Android
Package name: com.bigkidsapps.kidsbuddhaeng
Developer: Digital Buddha Apps (Big Kids Apps)

Effective date: January 1, 2024  ·  Last updated: September 19, 2026

For parents and guardians. This app is made for children and young people from age 0 to 18. We do not collect names, emails, photos, or accounts. We do not have a website. Advertising is child-directed, G-rated, Google AdMob only, and not personalized. For questions, email digitalbuddhaapps@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Digital Buddha Apps (“we,” “us”) publishes Kids Buddhist Songs (English), a free Android app with English Buddhist songs, lyrics, and pictures for children and families. This page is the public privacy policy for the app. It describes what information is handled when someone uses the app, including children.

We do not operate a company website. The only way to contact us is by email: digitalbuddhaapps@gmail.com.

2. Who this app is for

The app is designed for children and young people ages 0 through 18, and for parents or guardians who help them use it. It is a family music player. There is no in-app chat, no user accounts, no social feed, and no in-app purchases.

Because children under 13 may use the app, we follow the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families requirements. For advertising, we treat every user as a child, including teenagers.

3. Information we collect

We do not require registration. We do not ask for a name, email address, phone number, date of birth, or photo. Song audio, lyrics, and images are stored inside the app on the device.

We do not collect:

  • Names, contact details, or account logins
  • Precise GPS location
  • Camera, microphone, or contacts
  • Chat messages or user-generated posts
  • The Android Advertising ID (the app does not request the AD_ID permission)

The app may store a few settings only on the device (for example, that ads are hidden for 30 minutes after a parent watches an optional rewarded ad). Those settings are not sent to our servers. We do not run our own analytics or crash-reporting service.

Google’s advertising and consent tools may automatically process limited technical data, such as device type, operating system, language, IP address (used for approximate region, not precise GPS), and whether an ad was shown or tapped. See section 5.

If a parent emails us for support, we receive whatever the parent chooses to write in that email. We use it only to reply and then keep it only as long as needed for that request.

4. Children’s privacy (COPPA and Families Policy)

  • The app is directed to children ages 0–18.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
  • There is no child account, profile, or social feature inside the app.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • Ads are requested only as family-safe, child-directed ads (see below).
  • Optional YouTube, Facebook page, Play Store, share, and email buttons are meant for parents. They open outside the app.

A parent or guardian may email us with the subject COPPA Request to ask questions or to request that we help delete any advertising-related data Google may hold for this app, to the extent we can assist.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob only)

The free app is supported by ads. We use Google AdMob only. There is no ads mediation and no other ad network. Facebook Audience Network / Meta ads are not used. The Facebook button in the app is only a link to our public Facebook page for parents.

Family settings applied to every ad request:

  • Child-directed treatment (AgeRestrictedTreatment.CHILD and tag for child-directed treatment)
  • Maximum ad content rating G
  • Publisher ad personalization disabled
  • No personalized or interest-based ads for this child audience

Ads that may appear:

  • Native ads in a reserved 80dp slot, labeled “Ad,” with AdChoices. They appear on the song list footer and on the player under the subtitle, away from play buttons and Options.
  • App open ads when returning to the app from the background. They are not shown on first launch and not shown when moving between screens inside the app.
  • Optional rewarded video if someone taps Ad-Free. Watching a short ad can hide ads for 30 minutes. This is never shown automatically.

The app does not show interstitial ads on Back, on song change, or on launch. There is no “More Apps” ad button.

Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Consent in the EEA, United Kingdom, and Switzerland

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland we use Google’s certified Consent Management Platform (CMP): the User Messaging Platform (UMP), registered with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF).

  • Consent information is updated before the ads SDK starts and before any ad is requested.
  • Because this is a children’s app, UMP is requested with tag-for-under-age-of-consent.
  • Ads load only when the CMP reports that ads may be requested.
  • Where required, choices can be opened again from Privacy Policy → Privacy options inside the app.

Using the app is not treated as consent for personalized ads. This children’s app does not request personalized ads.

7. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load family-safe ads and, if a parent chooses, to open external links.
  • Set wallpaper: only if someone chooses Set as Wallpaper. The picture used is already inside the app.
  • Modify system settings: only if someone chooses Set as Ringtone, so the current song can be set as the device ringtone.

We do not request storage access for browsing the device, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, or precise location.

8. Third-party services and links

These third parties may process limited data when their tools are used:

  • Google AdMob: serves the ads described above.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): shows a consent message where required and stores choices (including a TC string) on the device.
  • Google Play: if a parent opens Rate This App.
  • YouTube and Facebook: only if a parent taps those toolbar links. Those sites have their own privacy policies. We do not embed social plugins or trackers for them inside the child experience.

9. How information is used, shared, and retained

We do not sell information. We do not use information for our own marketing lists. We have no user database on our servers.

Google may use limited technical data to deliver and measure family-safe ads, as described in Google’s policies. Device settings (such as an ad-free timer) stay on the device until the app is uninstalled. Android backup of app data is turned off.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask about, delete, or object to processing of personal information. Parents of children under 13 have additional COPPA rights. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, use Privacy options in the app when that control is shown, and contact us by email.

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
