Privacy Policy

Kids Buddha App

Developer: Big Kids Apps

Package Name: com.bigkidsapps.kidsbuddhaeng

Important Notice for Parents: This app is designed for children under 5 years old and adults. We take children's privacy very seriously and have implemented appropriate safeguards.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Our app does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information including:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Photos, videos, or audio recordings from your device
  • Personal messages or communications
  • Browsing history or search queries
  • Device identifiers that could be used to track you across apps
  • Any other personally identifiable information

1.2 Technical Information (Minimal)

The app may collect only the following technical information necessary for basic functionality:

  • App usage statistics (anonymized)
  • Device type and operating system version (for compatibility)
  • App performance data (for improving user experience)

2. Third-Party Services

2.1 Google AdMob

Our app displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Device advertising identifiers (which can be reset in device settings)
  • General location data (country/region level only)
  • App usage patterns for ad personalization

You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings or through AdMob's privacy controls.

2.2 No Other Third-Party Data Collection

We do not use any other third-party analytics, tracking, or data collection services.

3. How We Use Information

The minimal technical information we collect is used solely for:

  • Improving app performance and user experience
  • Fixing bugs and technical issues
  • Ensuring the app works properly on different devices

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect any technical data we collect:

  • Data is stored securely and encrypted when possible
  • Access to data is limited to authorized personnel only
  • We regularly review and update our security practices

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Special Protection for Children: Since our app is designed for children under 5, we have implemented additional privacy protections:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children
  • No social features or user-generated content
  • No in-app purchases or external links that could compromise privacy
  • All content is pre-loaded and does not require internet access for core functionality

6. App Permissions

Our app requests the following permissions and how we use them:

  • INTERNET & NETWORK ACCESS: Only for displaying ads and checking connectivity
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Only for saving ringtones (with user permission)
  • WRITE_SETTINGS: Only for setting ringtones (with user permission)
  • SET_WALLPAPER: Only for setting wallpapers (with user permission)
  • WAKE_LOCK: To prevent screen from turning off during audio playback

7. Data Sharing

We do NOT share, sell, or trade any information with third parties except:

  • Google AdMob (for advertising purposes only)
  • When required by law or legal process

8. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Opt out of personalized ads in your device settings
  • Reset your advertising identifier in device settings
  • Uninstall the app at any time (which removes all local data)
  • Contact us with privacy concerns (see contact information below)

9. International Users

This app is available internationally. If you are using the app from outside the United States, please note that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States where our servers are located.
