Privacy Policy for Kunzang Monlam

Last updated: January 2025

Kunzang Monlam is a spiritual Buddhist prayer application that respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information while providing a sacred space for spiritual practice.

As a spiritual application dedicated to Buddhist prayers and meditation, we approach data collection with mindfulness and respect for your spiritual journey.

1. Information We Collect

Kunzang Monlam is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information necessary for app functionality:

  • App Usage Data: Anonymous analytics to understand how users interact with our spiritual content
  • Device Information: Basic device details for compatibility and optimal performance
  • Audio Settings: Your audio preferences and equalizer settings for the karaoke feature
  • Language Preferences: Your selected language for prayers and content
  • Crash Reports: Technical information to improve app stability and performance

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information solely to enhance your spiritual experience:

  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Optimize audio quality for karaoke and prayer recitation
  • Provide better spiritual content and features
  • Fix technical issues and bugs
  • Ensure compatibility across different devices
  • Personalize content based on language preferences

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. We may share information only when:

  • Required by law or legal process
  • Necessary for app functionality and services
  • With your explicit consent
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Your spiritual practice and personal data are treated with the same care and respect.

5. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services to enhance functionality:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements to support app development
  • Google Analytics: For understanding app usage patterns (anonymized)
  • Facebook SDK: For social media integration and sharing features
  • Crash Reporting Services: For identifying and fixing technical issues

Advertising Notice: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. These ads help us maintain and improve the app. AdMob may collect and process data according to their own privacy policy.

6. Audio and Karaoke Features

Our app includes audio playback and karaoke features:

  • Audio files are stored locally on your device
  • Karaoke lyrics and timing data are processed locally
  • Audio enhancement settings (equalizer, bass boost) are saved locally
  • No audio content is transmitted to our servers

7. Children's Privacy

Kunzang Monlam is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our spiritual content is family-friendly and appropriate for users of all ages.

8. International Users

Our app is available in multiple languages and regions. If you are using the app outside your country of residence, please note that your information may be transferred to and processed in countries with different privacy laws.

9. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will notify users of any material changes through the app or our website.

10. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request data deletion
  • Opt-out of data collection (though this may affect app functionality)
  • Control advertising preferences through your device settings
  • Contact us with privacy concerns

11. Contact Information

For questions about this privacy policy or your spiritual app experience, contact us at:

Email: support@digitalbuddhaapps.com

Website: digitalbuddhaapps.com

12. Consent

By using Kunzang Monlam, you consent to this privacy policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

May this app serve as a tool for your spiritual practice and bring peace and wisdom to your journey. We are committed to maintaining the sacred nature of your spiritual experience while ensuring the security of your personal information.
