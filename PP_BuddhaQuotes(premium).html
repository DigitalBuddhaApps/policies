Privacy Policy – Buddha Quotes (Premium)

Digital Buddha Apps created Buddha Quotes (Premium) to share mindful teachings with a global audience, including families and children under 13. This document explains what information the app uses, how it is handled, and the limited moments when data might leave your device.

1. What This Policy Covers

This policy applies to the Buddha Quotes (Premium) Android application and any optional experiences that ship with it. We do not run user accounts or cloud services, so all reading happens entirely on your device.

2. Information Stored Locally

The app keeps only lightweight preferences inside Android Shared Preferences so that your reading experience feels consistent. These values never leave your phone unless you back up or export the device yourself.

  • Selected book language and translation
  • Preferred text size and interface options
  • Last opened chapter or navigation choices

3. When Information Leaves Your Device

We do not send personal data to our servers. Information only leaves the device when you choose to interact with an external app, for example:

  • Opening Google Play to rate or discover other Digital Buddha Apps titles
  • Sending us an email at digitalbuddhaapps@gmail.com
  • Sharing a link to the app with friends

4. Target Audience Includes Children Under 13

Buddha Quotes (Premium) is designed for general audiences and explicitly welcomes children under 13 with supervision from parents, guardians, or educators. Because we do not collect personal information, the experience complies with the core expectations of COPPA and similar child-safety regulations. We also avoid push notifications, social logins, or behavioral advertising.

5. Third-Party Services

The current build uses only Google Play Services components that are required for distribution (such as billing or licensing) and standard Android frameworks. If we ever integrate Google AdMob or analytics, it will be limited to contextual, family-safe content and disclosed in an updated version of this document.

6. Your Choices & Controls

  • You can clear the app’s data from Android Settings to remove every locally stored preference.
  • You may uninstall the app at any time. We do not keep residual copies of your usage data.
  • If we release material updates, we will highlight them in the in-app privacy dialog and on our Google Play listing.

7. Contact Us

Questions about privacy, accessibility, or child safety? Email digitalbuddhaapps@gmail.com. We typically respond within 72 hours.
