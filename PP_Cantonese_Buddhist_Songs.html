IMPORTANT NOTICE: This app is designed for a general audience, including children under 13 years of age. We are fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and do not collect any personal information from users of any age.

1. Information We Collect

We DO NOT collect any personal information.

Buddhist Songs (Cantonese) does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information from users, including:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Physical addresses
  • Geolocation data
  • Photos or videos
  • Contacts
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, etc.)
  • IP addresses
  • Browsing history
  • Any other personal information

2. COPPA Compliance (Children Under 13)

This app is specifically designed to be safe for children under 13 years of age. We comply with all Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) requirements:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect any personal information from children or adults
  • Child-Directed Treatment: All advertisements shown through Google AdMob are configured for child-directed treatment
  • Non-Personalized Ads Only: We only show non-personalized, age-appropriate advertisements that comply with COPPA
  • No Behavioral Tracking: We do not track user behavior, create user profiles, or engage in any form of behavioral advertising
  • No Third-Party Data Sharing: We do not share any data with third parties except for anonymous ad delivery
  • Parental Consent Not Required: Since we collect no personal information whatsoever, parental consent is not required under COPPA
  • No User Accounts: The app does not require or create user accounts
  • No Social Media Integration: No social media login or sharing capabilities that could collect personal data

3. Advertising

Buddhist Songs (Cantonese) displays advertisements through Google AdMob to support free access to Buddhist songs and chants. Our advertising is fully COPPA-compliant:

  • Non-Personalized Ads: All advertisements are strictly non-personalized and do not use cookies, device identifiers, or any tracking technology for ad targeting
  • Age-Appropriate Content: Ads are restricted to General Audiences (rated G) content only, suitable for all ages
  • Child-Directed Treatment: Google AdMob is configured to treat all users as children under 13, ensuring COPPA compliance
  • Limited Data Collection: Google AdMob may collect minimal technical information (device type, app version) solely for ad delivery purposes, but this data:
    • Does not include personal information
    • Cannot be used to identify individual users
    • Is not used for personalized advertising
    • Is not shared with other advertisers
  • Frequency Capping: Ads are shown with appropriate frequency limits to provide a better user experience, especially for children

For more information about Google AdMob's privacy practices and COPPA compliance, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used in this app is Google AdMob for advertisements. Important details:

  • Google AdMob is configured with child-directed treatment settings
  • COPPA compliance settings are enabled
  • Maximum ad content rating is set to General Audiences (G)
  • Personalized advertising is completely disabled
  • No other third-party services, analytics, or tracking tools are used

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect any personal information, there is no personal data to secure, store, or protect. The app:

  • Functions entirely offline except when displaying advertisements
  • Does not maintain any user databases
  • Does not store any user data on our servers (we don't have any servers)
  • Does not cache or store any user-generated content

6. Internet Permissions

Buddhist Songs (Cantonese) requires internet permission only for the following purposes:

  • Displaying advertisements through Google AdMob
  • No other data is transmitted over the internet
  • No analytics or crash reporting data is sent
  • No user behavior data is transmitted

7. App Permissions

The app requires the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET: Required for displaying ads only
  • SET_WALLPAPER & SET_WALLPAPER_HINTS: Optional feature allowing users to set Buddhist images as wallpaper (no data collection)

The app does NOT request or use permissions for:

  • Camera access
  • Microphone access
  • Location services
  • Contact list access
  • Storage access (except for wallpaper feature)
  • Phone state or call information
